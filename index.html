Privacy Policy

Data Unit Converter · Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Data Unit Converter mobile application ("the App") handles your information. Your privacy is important, and the App is designed to respect it completely.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The App does not require an account, does not ask for any personal information, and does not use any analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your preferences — such as your chosen theme, calculation mode, decimal precision and default units — locally on your device so they are remembered the next time you open the App. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App removes it permanently.

Internet & Permissions

The App works fully offline and does not request internet access or any sensitive device permissions.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
